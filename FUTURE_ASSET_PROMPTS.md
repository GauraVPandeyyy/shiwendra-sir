# Future asset direction

Priority is genuine supplied photography. These are capture briefs and optional illustrative directions, not instructions to fabricate documentary evidence.

## 1. Genuine public-life sequence — highest value

Commission 3–5 high-resolution photographs of actual public conversations or field visits, with consent and correct location/date records. Capture one wide contextual frame, one medium interaction and one quiet detail. Leave useful negative space; avoid staged claims or invented participation. Select 1–3 additional non-event images for Home and a broader real-media collection for Gallery.

## 2. Harchandpur-specific contextual photography

Photograph actual roads, water-management infrastructure, agricultural settings and public-service access points. Record the exact place and date privately. Use captions that describe what is visible; do not suggest that a problem or improvement has been independently established by a photograph alone. These can replace abstract road/water/farmer chapter illustrations after review.

## 3. Authorized high-resolution inspiration portraits

Obtain higher-resolution portraits of Kanshi Ram and Mayawati through an authorized archive or official rights holder. Keep the original source, license and credit. Do not create synthetic faces or AI “restorations” that invent details. Current sources are usable archival placeholders with the limitations noted in ASSET_SOURCES.md.

## 4. Optional non-documentary service illustration

If new thematic imagery is needed: “Quiet editorial illustration of access to education or healthcare in an Indian community; natural daylight, dignified human scale, restrained blue and warm neutral palette, space for live HTML text, no political symbols, no recognizable public figures, no embedded lettering, no suggestion of a specific documented event.” Label representative imagery visibly and never place it in documentary Gallery.

## 5. Genuine accessible video

Capture a short approved public message or a real non-excluded activity. Supply a clean local MP4, still poster, verified date/context, Hindi captions and a reviewed English translation/transcript. No autoplay audio and no recreated event footage.
